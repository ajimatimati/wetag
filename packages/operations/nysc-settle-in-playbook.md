# weTag NYSC Settle-In Outreach & Housing Playbook

*Helping National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) Members Settle into Ibadan Stress-Free*

---

## 1. The Corps Member Problem
Every year, thousands of youth corps members are posted to Oyo State (Iseyin Orientation Camp) and redeployed into Ibadan for their Place of Primary Assignment (PPA) across schools, ministries, hospitals, and private firms.

They face:
1. **Opaque "Total Package" pricing**: Landlords and roadside agents charging up to ₦600k for sub-standard rooms.
2. **Long, dangerous commutes**: Living far from their PPA without reliable transit.
3. **Isolation**: Struggling to find safe, like-minded flatmates to split living expenses.

---

## 2. NYSC Settle-In Campaign Strategy

### Step 1: Orientation Camp Outreach (Iseyin Camp Passing Out Parade)
- Distribution of digital QR cards: *"Find your place near your PPA with vetted flatmates & no hidden fees."*
- Dedicated NYSC Onboarding portal in the app.

### Step 2: PPA Proximity & Commute Search
- Corps members select their assigned PPA (e.g. *University of Ibadan, Oyo State Secretariat, UCH, Federal High Court*).
- System queries listings within a **15–25 minute commute radius** along active MOVE carpool corridors.

### Step 3: Flatmate Matching & Household Ledger
- Flatmate preference match: Pairing compatible corps members (same gender, early risers, study-focused).
- Household Creation: Automated 50/50 or 33/33/34 rent split and digital shared utility ledger for IBEDC electricity and water.
