# weTag Property Verification Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

*Quality & Anti-Fraud Standards for Ibadan Housing Inventory*

---

## 1. Objective
Ensure every listing on weTag has been physically inspected, its fees itemized with zero unadvertised costs, and its Nigerian infrastructure reality validated.

---

## 2. Mandatory On-Site Inspection Checklist

### A. Infrastructure & Utilities
- [ ] **Water Supply**: Test borehole tap. Check for steady flow, clarity, and storage overhead tank. Note if water is well-based or municipal.
- [ ] **Electricity & Metering**:
  - Verify if meter is **Dedicated Prepaid (IBEDC)** or shared postpaid disc.
  - Document meter number to prevent phantom meter claims.
- [ ] **Generator & Alternative Power**:
  - Verify estate or building generator schedule (e.g. 7 PM – 6 AM).
  - Note presence of solar inverter systems.
- [ ] **Drainage & Flood History**: Inspect compound drainage channel slope and road access during rainy season.

### B. Price & Fee Itemization Audit
Inspectors must meet the property owner or registered managing agent directly to confirm:
- **Base Annual Rent**: ₦X
- **Agency Fee**: Maximum platform standard 10% (₦Y)
- **Legal Agreement Fee**: Maximum platform standard 10% (₦Z)
- **Caution / Refundable Deposit**: Fixed amount (₦C)
- **Service Charge / Security Dues**: Fixed annual or monthly amount.

*If any unlisted fee is required by the agent on site, the listing is rejected.*

---

## 3. Image Integrity & Anti-Recycling Protocol
1. Inspectors capture **original wide-angle photos** on site with GPS geolocation tags enabled.
2. System computes 64-bit perceptual image hashes (`pHash`) to ensure photos have never been scraped from third-party social media classifieds.
3. Once approved, the listing receives the **"Verified by weTag"** green shield badge.
