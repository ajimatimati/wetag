# weTag Driver Supply Acquisition & Onboarding Playbook

*Phase 1 Target: 100 Verified Commuter Drivers across Core Ibadan Corridors*

---

## 1. Target Driver Persona
weTag does **not** recruit full-time taxi drivers on empty patrol runs. We target **everyday working professionals, university staff, and healthcare workers** who already commute daily:
- **Akobo ➔ Dugbe**: Corporate professionals, bankers at Dugbe Commercial District.
- **UI ➔ Secretariat**: Lecturers, administrative officers, civil servants at Agodi.
- **Bodija ➔ Ring Road**: Lawyers, judicial workers, business owners.

---

## 2. Driver Onboarding Funnel & Conversion

```
1. Outreach (Corporate Hubs / UI)
   └── 2. Self-Service Signup (Phone OTP + Profile)
       └── 3. Smile ID KYC (NIN + Driver's License)
           └── 4. Physical Vehicle Inspection (5-min checklist)
               └── 5. 10-Min Briefing (PIN protocol + Earnings)
                   └── 6. Account Activation & First Shared Commute
```

---

## 3. Physical Vehicle Inspection Protocol (5-Minute Check)
Every vehicle must pass physical verification before activation:
1. **Exterior & Identity**:
   - License plate matches registered plate (`make`, `model`, `year`, `color`).
   - Valid vehicle license and roadworthiness sticker on windscreen.
2. **Safety Systems**:
   - Functional 3-point seatbelts on all passenger seats.
   - Tires with visible tread depth (no worn-out or bald tires).
   - Working headlights, indicators, brake lights, and hazard lights.
3. **Comfort Standards**:
   - Functional air conditioning (mandatory for AC-tagged commutes).
   - Clean, odor-free interior upholstery with clear boot space.

---

## 4. Driver Economics & Fuel Offset Pitch
- **Average Commute**: Akobo ➔ Dugbe (14 km round trip = ~₦2,200 fuel daily).
- **With 2 Passengers**: ₦400/seat x 2 = ₦800 per trip = ₦1,600 daily offset (72% of daily fuel cost).
- **Monthly Net Benefit**: ~**₦35,000 to ₦48,000** back in the commuter's pocket without extra driving hours or lifestyle change.
