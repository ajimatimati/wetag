## 2. Overcoming Common Objections

### Objection 1: "I don't want strangers in my private car."
> **Response**: *"Totally understand! That's why weTag is completely different from commercial taxis. Every single passenger must verify their National Identity Number (NIN) and phone number. You see their employer/university profile and rating before accepting, and you choose who gets in."*

### Objection 2: "Will this delay me from getting to work?"
> **Response**: *"Not at all. You set a strict detour budget — for example, 'maximum 5 minutes detour' or 'smart hub pickup only'. The app only matches colleagues standing directly along your natural driving corridor (like General Gas junction or Total station)."*

### Objection 3: "How do I get paid?"
> **Response**: *"Passengers pay directly inside the app before boarding. As soon as the ride ends and you drop them off, the funds land in your weTag wallet and can be transferred straight to your GTBank, Access, Zenith or any Nigerian bank account instantly via Paystack."*

## 3. Onboarding & 5-Minute Physical Inspection Protocol
1. **Phone Registration**: Guide the driver to `https://wetag.ng` or download the app.
2. **KYC Upload**: Snap the driver's license and National Identity card.
3. **Vehicle Check**:
   - Inspect tires, wipers, seatbelts, and working air conditioning.
   - Confirm plate number matches vehicle papers.
4. **First Corridor Publication**: Help the driver publish their first recurring commute (e.g. *Monday–Friday, 7:30 AM, Akobo ➔ Dugbe, 2 empty seats offered*).
5. **Issue Driver Welcome Kit**: Give driver the weTag high-contrast sunshade card and emergency hotline sticker.
