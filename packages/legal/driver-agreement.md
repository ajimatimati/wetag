# weTag Driver Participation & Cost-Sharing Agreement

*For Commuter & Earner Participants in Oyo State*

---

## 1. Status of the Driver
By registering your vehicle on weTag, you acknowledge that you are participating as an **Independent Commuter / Peer-to-Peer Cost-Sharing Earner**. This Agreement does **not** establish an employment relationship, agency, joint venture, or franchise with weTag.

---

## 2. Vehicle Requirements & Roadworthiness
1. **Registered Vehicle**: You must provide valid registration papers for the specific vehicle listed on your profile (`make`, `model`, `year`, `color`, `plateNumber`).
2. **No Vehicle Swapping**: Commencing a journey with a vehicle other than the approved vehicle associated with your profile is a severe safety breach and results in immediate account freeze.
3. **Condition**: Vehicles must have functioning seatbelts for all passengers, working air conditioning (if AC flag is active), functional brakes, tires with adequate tread depth, and current vehicle inspection certification.

---

## 3. Mandatory Safety Protocols
1. **4-Digit PIN Validation**: You must verify and enter the rider's 4-digit PIN upon arrival at the pickup point before departing.
2. **GPS Streaming**: You agree to keep the weTag application running in the foreground or background during active trips to enable live route tracking and safety anomaly detection.
3. **Nighttime Safety Curfew Compliance**: You agree to abide by all Oyo State nocturnal traffic and transport regulations.

---

## 4. Financial Structure & Payouts
1. **Fare Formula**: Contributions are calculated automatically based on route distance and seat allocation. You agree to accept the platform-calculated amount without soliciting surcharges or tips.
2. **Platform Commission**: weTag deducts a 10% platform facilitation fee and a fixed ₦50 safety reserve contribution per completed seat.
3. **Disbursement**: Your net earnings are credited to your Universal Wallet immediately upon trip completion and may be withdrawn to your verified Nigerian commercial bank account at any time.
