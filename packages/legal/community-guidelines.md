# weTag Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

*Respect · Transparency · Safety in Ibadan*

weTag is built on mutual respect and shared neighborhood trust. These guidelines apply equally to riders, drivers, tenants, landlords, and property agents.

---

## 1. Zero Tolerance Violations (Immediate Permanent Ban)
- **Violence or Physical Assault**: Any threat, physical aggression, or harassment results in immediate account deactivation and criminal referral to the Oyo State Police Command.
- **Sexual Misconduct**: Unwanted romantic advances, inappropriate comments on appearance, or physical contact of any sexual nature are strictly prohibited.
- **Discrimination & Bigotry**: Refusal of service, abusive language, or discriminatory housing restrictions based on ethnicity, religion, gender, or state of origin is not tolerated.
- **Fraud & Extortion**: Posting fake property listings, taking unapproved offline cash payments, or vehicle swap scams.

---

## 2. Shared Commute Etiquette (MOVE)
- **Punctuality**: Be at the pickup hub or door location before the driver arrives. Commuters are heading to work; delays affect everyone in the vehicle.
- **Cleanliness & Comfort**: Respect the private vehicle. No smoking, vaping, or consuming pungent food inside the car.
- **Personal Space & Conversation**: Respect your co-riders' ride preferences (e.g. `Quiet Ride` toggle).
- **Luggage**: Only bring baggage that fits comfortably in the boot without encroaching on fellow passengers' legroom.

---

## 3. Housing & Co-Living Etiquette (STAY)
- **Honesty in Listings**: Real Move-In Totals must represent 100% of required fees. No surprise inspection fees at the gate.
- **Viewing Respect**: Show up on time for booked viewing appointments or cancel with at least 2 hours notice.
- **Roommate Harmony**: Honor agreed household rules regarding noise hours, overnight guests, cleaning rotations, and prompt settlement of shared utility bills.

---

## 4. Reporting & Dispute Resolution
If an incident occurs, use the **Safety Pill** (SOS) during a trip or report via the **In-App Support Centre**. Every report is reviewed by human safety operators within 15 minutes.
